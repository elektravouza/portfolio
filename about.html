Hey there, welcome to my space. I am Ilektra Vouza, and I am just a girl — you can just call me Ilektra. I am a multidisciplinary designer, creator, and researcher working across art, science, and technology.

I studied Mathematics and Computational Theory at the University of Patras, and later specialized in Digital Design through postgraduate studies at Vakalo Art & Design College. This combination of logic and design continues to shape how I think, build, and explore.

I move between UI/UX design, generative systems, and interactive installations, alongside experimental digital experiences. I am especially interested in how complex systems can be translated into sensory and participatory forms, where interaction becomes a way of thinking rather than a functional layer of use.

With a background in mathematics and cultural theory, I approach creation as a form of research and a method for engaging with complex systems and contemporary realities. My practice is driven by curiosity and a continuous commitment to interdisciplinary thinking, where critical inquiry and experimentation resist fixed boundaries.

I bridge industry practice with research-based and experimental work, developing projects that operate both as functional design solutions and conceptual explorations. For me, design is a reflective medium for generating new forms of understanding — drawn to systems that are not fixed but evolving, where design becomes a form of inquiry.

My work has been exhibited in different contexts, including Hackney Gallery in London, The Holy Art Gallery in Athens, and ADAF Festival in Athens — each experience contributing to how I understand space, audience, and interaction.

The Core Inquiry

"How do we think through systems and how do systems think through us?"
