Hey there, welcome to my space. I am Ilektra Vouza, and I am just a girl. You can just call me Ilektra. I studied Mathematics and Computational Theory at the University of Patras, and later specialized in Digital Design through postgraduate studies at Vakalo Art & Design College. This combination of logic and design continues to shape how I think, build, and explore.

Approach & Philosophy

I define myself as a creator and researcher across art, science, and technology. With a background in mathematics and cultural theory, I approach creation as a form of research and a method for engaging with complex systems and contemporary realities. My practice is driven by curiosity and a continuous commitment to interdisciplinary thinking, where critical inquiry and experimentation resist fixed boundaries.

I move between UI/UX design, generative systems, interactive installations, and experimental digital experiences. I am especially interested in how complex systems can be translated into sensory and participatory forms, where interaction becomes a way of thinking rather than a functional layer of use.

Practice & Context

I bridge industry practice with research-based and experimental work, developing projects that operate both as functional design solutions and conceptual explorations. For me, design is a reflective medium for generating new forms of understanding and engaging with complex societal, technological, and cultural conditions.

Across my practice, I am drawn to systems that are not fixed but evolving, where design becomes a form of inquiry. I am interested in how digital environments can hold uncertainty, provoke reflection, and invite users into processes of decision-making and meaning construction.

Professional & Academic

Alongside my creative practice, I work in a digital creative agency as a design team lead, where I develop websites, digital products, and interactive platforms, while also contributing to creative direction and team development.

I also teach and assist in academic contexts, focusing on design theory and research methodologies, which allows me to remain in constant dialogue with emerging ideas and practitioners.

Exhibitions & Research

I have developed independent research-driven projects that explore technology, behavior, and ethics through interactive systems. These works have been presented in academic and cultural contexts, including conferences, lectures, and collaborative artistic spaces.

My work has been exhibited in different contexts, including Hackney Gallery in London, The Holy Art Gallery in Athens, and ADAF Festival in Athens — each experience contributing to how I understand space, audience, and interaction.

The Core Inquiry

"How do we think through systems and how do systems think through us?"
